How the tree works: Categories → Groups → Types. A “group” is just an asset_types row with no parent; anything with parent_type_id set appears under it. A parentless row that gains children silently becomes a group (and disappears from the finder search); a parentless row with no children shows as an empty group. Edits and deletes here are audited with before/after snapshots — admins can undo them from the Audit Log.
