? HOW THIS WORKS click to expand

Window. Each performance's start is its date + time from the advance form. The end is the end time of the production-schedule row on that day that starts at the performance time (e.g. 19:30–22:15 Show); without one, start + the default show length. With curtain detection on, Spot looks for the real curtain up/down within ± the search window and reports on those.

When. Every 5 minutes the cluster's primary server (only one server, never all of them) pulls every performance that ended more than the grace period ago — plus the detection window when detection is on, so a late curtain-down is in the data. A pulled performance is not pulled again unless its times or sensors change. Failures retry with back-off up to the attempt limit.

Where it lands. The numbers (Leq, Max, L10/L50/L90, Avg per sensor) show in the show's Post Show tab and print on the Post-Show Notes PDF; Spot's PDF report is filed under the show's Files (a re-pull archives the earlier report). Shows marked test/demo are skipped.
